# Silk Bored

**Certified excuses from Bengaluru's finest junctions.** A tilt-shift diorama of a jammed junction (autos, BMTC buses, rooftop water tanks, a metro "coming 2027*") where the traffic gets worse as you drag the "how late" slider. Pick your junction, who's waiting and a tone (Corporate, Desperate, Poetic, Legal notice, Swalpa Kannada), then:

- **Generate:** hand-written templates, free and instant (`public/excuses.js`).
- **Bulletproof it:** a cheap AI model writes a custom one (`api/excuse.js`). Rate-limited, and it keeps 4 cached variants per combination.
- **Certificate:** a downloadable "Traffic Delay Certificate" from the Bureau of Bumper-to-Bumper Affairs, drawn on a canvas. It says right on it that it has no legal value.
- **Share:** copy, WhatsApp, or the PNG.

The diorama is a toy simulation, not live traffic, and the page says so.
